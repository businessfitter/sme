What Orzio Commerce does

Stores and channels

Your own website with a live shop widget, Shopify and WooCommerce connections, and marketplace order files.

Live stock everywhere

Order queue

Fulfilment

Questions e-commerce ask us

Do we have to leave Shopify?

No. Keep your Shopify or WooCommerce store; Orzio takes the orders and pushes stock back.

Can we sell on Amazon and Noon?

Yes. Import their order files and export listings and stock.

How fast does stock update on our website?

The shop widget refreshes every 20 seconds; Shopify and WooCommerce are updated after each shipment or receipt.

Can we run shops as well?

Yes. Sales points in each branch use the same stock and close into the books daily.
