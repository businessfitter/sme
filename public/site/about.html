Why we built Orzio

Small and medium businesses everywhere run on spreadsheets, a separate tool for every department and a finance team that types the same numbers three times.

We are finance people. We have closed books for insurers, trading houses, clinics and family groups, and we have seen how much of the work is preparation: matching, copying, chasing and checking. That work is exactly what AI agents are good at.

Orzio is built around one idea: let agents prepare the work, and let people decide. Every entry, payment, policy and message an agent drafts comes with its reasoning, and waits for the person who is allowed to approve it.

What we believe

  • One suite with one ledger beats seven tools stitched together.
  • Controls belong in the database, not in a training manual.
  • A clinic, an insurance broker and an online brand need different modules, not different software.
  • Software should speak plainly, in your language and your country’s rules.

Where we are

Orzio is built in the United Arab Emirates and serves businesses worldwide, each set up for its own country and industry. Talk to us at hello@orzio.ai.
