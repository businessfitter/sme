Questions consultancies ask us

Can we bill a retainer and extra hours?

Yes. Retainers bill monthly and work outside scope is logged and billed as time.

Can clients approve timesheets?

Clients can approve statements and sign documents in the portal; detailed time stays internal unless you share it.

How do partners see profitability?

Each engagement shows hours, billed, WIP, realisation and margin after salary cost.

Do associates and subcontractors fit?

Yes. Their fees post to cost of revenue against the engagement.
