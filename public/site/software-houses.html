Questions software houses ask us

Do you replace Stripe or our billing provider?

You can keep your payment provider for card collection. Orzio holds the subscription records, invoices and revenue recognition.

Can we bill in several currencies?

Yes. Each company has a base currency, and invoices can be raised in the customer’s currency with exchange differences handled.

How are implementation fees treated?

Distinct services are recognised as delivered; set-up fees that are not distinct are spread over the subscription, as your revenue policy says.

Do we need a separate project tool?

For billing and time, no. Many teams keep their engineering tracker and log billable time in Orzio.
