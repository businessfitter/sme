Questions holding companies ask us

Do subsidiaries need their own Orzio?

No. They are companies inside your workspace. Their staff can be given access to their own company only.

Can companies use different currencies?

Yes. Each keeps its own currency; the group is translated at closing, average and historical rates.

Who can see the consolidated group?

Only people you allow in HQ access. Company-level staff see their own company.

Can we include investments that are not in Orzio?

Yes. Add them to the portfolio with their cost and value; only companies in Orzio are consolidated line by line.
