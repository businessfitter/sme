Trading and distribution

Importers, wholesalers and distributors with stock in more than one place.

  • Procurement with supplier comparison and landed costs
  • Inventory across warehouses with transfers and counts
  • Wholesale price lists and quantity breaks
  • Customer statements, credit limits and collections
  • Corporate tax and VAT for each country

E-commerce and retail

Online brands, marketplace sellers and shops with branches.

  • Your website with live stock and checkout, plus Shopify and WooCommerce
  • Amazon and Noon orders imported, listings and stock exported
  • Pick, pack and ship with courier rate cards and tracking
  • Cash on delivery tracked until the courier pays it over
  • Sales points in each branch, closed daily into the books

Clinics and welfare societies

Clinics, dispensaries and charitable hospitals, including free and subsidised patients.

  • Front desk with appointments, online booking and tokens
  • Doctor’s desk: vitals, ICD-10 diagnosis and e-prescriptions in English and Urdu
  • Pharmacy with batches and expiry, and a laboratory with normal ranges
  • Welfare assessments, Zakat and donation funds
  • Donor dashboards that expire after 7 days

Professional services

Consultants, agencies, IT and engineering firms billing by project.

  • Quotes and service agreements signed online
  • Projects with timesheets, milestones and retention
  • Revenue over time or at a point in time
  • Payroll and billable staff costs
  • Partner dashboards for each client

Holdings and family groups

Groups with several companies, subsidiaries and countries.

  • One HQ with every company, subsidiary and branch
  • Consolidation with eliminations and currency translation
  • Non-controlling interests and investment eliminations
  • HQ and company-level access rights
  • UBO register and compliance for each company
