Questions insurance brokers ask us

Does Orzio replace our insurers’ portals?

No. You still bind with each insurer. Orzio keeps the client file, the money and the renewals in one place and does the accounting.

Can we keep premiums in our office account?

You can switch the client money account off, but most regulators require segregation. The check on the Premiums page shows the position either way.

How is commission recognised?

When the policy is placed and the debit note issued, net of any commission returned on endorsements, as IFRS 15 requires for an agent.

Can account executives see only their clients?

Roles decide what each person can do. Account executives work placements and policies; finance handles remittances.
