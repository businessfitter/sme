Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Who we are

Orzio provides business software. For the data you put into Orzio about your business, your customers, suppliers and employees, you are the controller and we process it for you. For the details of people who use our website and accounts, we are the controller. Contact: hello@orzio.ai.

What we collect

Account details: name, email and the workspaces you belong to. Usage: sign-ins, features used and agent tasks, to run and bill the service. Website: messages you send us through the contact form. Your business data: whatever you and your team enter or upload.

How we use it

To provide and secure the service, to support you, to bill you, and to tell you about changes. We do not sell personal data and we do not use your business data to train AI models.

Who processes it

Our cloud database and hosting providers, our email provider and our AI model provider act as sub-processors under contracts that protect your data. A current list is available on request.

Where it is stored

In the cloud region chosen for your workspace. Dedicated installations can be placed in a region that suits your regulator.

How long we keep it

While your subscription is active and for a limited period afterwards so you can export it, unless the law requires longer. You can ask us to delete a workspace.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete personal data about you, and object to some uses, under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, the GDPR where it applies, and other local laws. Write to hello@orzio.ai.
