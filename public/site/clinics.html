What Orzio Clinic does

On top of the full Orzio suite: accounting, sales, procurement, HR, compliance, documents and the help desk.

Front desk

Appointments, online booking requests, tokens and a waiting-room screen. Returning patients found by phone or MR number.

Doctor’s desk

Vitals, ICD-10 diagnosis, e-prescriptions with instructions in English and Urdu, tests and procedures, notes drafted by the agent.

Prescription checks

Allergies, duplicates, pregnancy and child dosing checked against the patient’s record before printing.

Pharmacy

A starter formulary, batches and expiry dates, first-expiry-first-out dispensing and the cost of every medicine given.

Laboratory

Orders from the doctor, results with normal ranges and flags, printed reports.

Billing and cash counter

Itemised bills, panel and insurance, card and wallet payments, day closing with a cash count.

Welfare and charity

Assessments approved by a committee, Zakat and donation funds applied automatically to free and subsidised patients.

Donor dashboards

Share what a donation paid for through a link that expires after 7 days, without exposing patient details.

Clinic reports

ICD-10 analysis, notifiable diseases, missed follow-ups, doctor fee shares and fund usage.

How the work flows

1

Register

The front desk registers the patient, takes the fee or applies the charity fund, and issues a token.

2

Consult

The doctor records vitals and diagnosis and writes the prescription; the agent checks it against the record.

3

Dispense and test

The pharmacy dispenses by batch and expiry; the laboratory records results against the order.

4

Close the day

Cash is counted, bills and funds post to the books, and donors see what their money paid for.

Questions clinics ask us

Can we run free and paid patients together?

Yes. Each bill shows what the patient pays and what a charity fund covers. Funds are tracked by donor and purpose.

Do we need internet at the front desk?

Orzio runs in the browser and on phones. A short loss of connection does not lose what was typed; it saves when the line returns.

Is patient data shared with donors?

No. Donor links show totals, counts and anonymous stories you choose, never names or diagnoses.

Does it handle several branches?

Yes. Each branch has its own front desk, pharmacy stock and cash counter, with one set of group accounts.
