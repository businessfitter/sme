How we protect your data

Questions owners ask us

Do the agents post anything without us?

Only what you allow. By default, entries above your approval limit, payments, payroll, eliminations and anything sent to customers wait for a person. You can let small, routine items post on their own.

Can we move from QuickBooks, Zoho, Tally or Excel?

Yes. Import your chart of accounts, opening balances, customers, suppliers, items and employees from Excel or CSV. We help with the first import on every paid plan.

Where is our data stored?

On managed cloud servers in the region you choose. Larger and regulated businesses can have a dedicated installation of their own.

We are a group with companies in different countries. Does it work?

Yes. Each company keeps its own currency, tax rules and books. The group view consolidates them with eliminations and currency translation.

We are accountants with many clients. How do we work?

Each client is its own workspace. Your team switches between them with one login, and each client only ever sees their own business. See the partner programme.
