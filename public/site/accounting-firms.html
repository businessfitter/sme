Questions accounting practices ask us

Where do the deadlines come from?

From the industry pack for your country, prepared by Orzio and approved by you. If your country is not covered yet, standard deadlines are used until it is.

Can we do the clients’ bookkeeping in Orzio too?

Yes. Each client can have its own workspace, which your team opens with the same login. See the partner programme.

Do clients see our board?

No. Clients see only their own workspace and the documents you request from them.

Does it handle audit work?

Audit engagements run as jobs with independence checks, budgets and review sign-off. Working papers stay in your audit tool or in Documents.
